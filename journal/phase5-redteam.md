# Phase 5 Implications — Post-Implementation Self-Review

## What could go wrong?

### Weakness 1: Self-audit bias
We wrote the implications AND we judge their severity.
A stakeholder we missed has no voice.
Mitigation: Phase 1 pilot includes stakeholder interviews.

### Weakness 2: No quantitative bias measurement
We claim Layer 1b may misclassify Asian crops but haven't measured it.
Mitigation: document as HIGH severity; tests to be added in Phase 1.

### Weakness 3: Workforce impact analysis is optimistic
We claim "skill upgrade for workers" but workers have no choice
in the matter. Automation anxiety is real even if jobs are preserved.
Mitigation: explicit worker consultation before Phase 1 deployment.

### Weakness 4: No mechanism for ongoing implications review
Implications can emerge over time as system matures.
Mitigation: quarterly audit scheduled in Gate 4 renewal criteria.

## What we learned
- Layer 1b PlantVillage bias (Western crop dataset) is a real HIGH risk
  that affects our actual demo (Asian crops)
- Admin mode password-gating for Layer 5 tone selector is genuine
  risk mitigation, not just a nice-to-have
- "Net positive for 5 of 6 stakeholders" is honest accounting
  — farm workers face genuine trade-offs

## Recommendations for Phase 1
- Hire/consult with an ethicist for the pilot review
- Implement worker survey at months 3, 6, 9 of pilot
- Publish implications audit quarterly (transparency)
