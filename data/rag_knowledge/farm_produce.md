# GreenLoop Farm Produce Guide

## Overview

GreenLoop operates a 3-layer hydroponic vertical farm in Singapore, producing premium leafy greens and herbs year-round using 95% less water than conventional farming.

## Crops We Grow

### Leafy Greens

- **Spinach (Spinacia oleracea)** — Rich in iron and vitamins A, C, K. Harvest: 25-30 days. Optimal EC: 1.8-2.3 mS/cm.
- **Kai Lan (Chinese broccoli)** — Popular in Singapore cuisine, high in vitamins A and C. Harvest: 40-50 days. Optimal temp: 18-24°C.
- **Arugula (Eruca vesicaria)** — Peppery leaf, high in nitrates and antioxidants. Harvest: 20-25 days. Optimal EC: 1.2-1.8 mS/cm.
- **Lettuce (Lactuca sativa)** — Multiple varieties including butterhead and romaine. Harvest: 28-35 days.
- **Kale (Brassica napus)** — Extremely high in vitamins K, A, C. Harvest: 30-40 days. Tolerates cooler temperatures.
- **Basil (Ocimum basilicum)** — Premium herb, high demand from restaurants. Harvest: 21-28 days. Requires warmer temperatures (22-28°C).
- **Cilantro (Coriandrum sativum)** — Popular herb with strong market demand. Harvest: 21-30 days.

### Growing Conditions

- **Light:** 16-18 hours/day of LED grow lights, PPFD 400-600 µmol/m²/s
- **Nutrient Solution:** Recirculating NFT (Nutrient Film Technique) with pH 5.8-6.4
- **Temperature:** 18-26°C ambient, 70-80% relative humidity
- **Water Usage:** 95% less than conventional farming — ~2L/kg vs 20L/kg
- **No pesticides** — Integrated pest management (IPM) with beneficial insects

## Harvest & Post-Harvest

- Harvested at peak freshness — typically same-day delivery to restaurants
- Hydroponically grown produce has 30% longer shelf life than soil-grown
- Cool chain maintained at 4°C from harvest to delivery
- Packaging: compostable bioplastic containers, no plastic wrap

## Seasonal Availability

All crops are available year-round due to controlled environment agriculture (CEA). No seasonal gaps — GreenLoop guarantees 52-week supply continuity.

## Why Hydroponic?

- No soil-borne diseases — zero pesticide residue
- 20x higher yield per square meter vs conventional farming
- Located 15km from Singapore CBD — ultra-fresh delivery within 2 hours of harvest
- Carbon footprint 60% lower than imported produce (avoids air freight)
