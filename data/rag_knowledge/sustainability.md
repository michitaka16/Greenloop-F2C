## Environmental Impact

### Water Conservation

Adopt a Kale's hydroponic system uses **95% less water** than conventional Singapore soil farming:

- Conventional farming: ~20 litres per kg of produce
- Adopt a Kale hydroponic: ~2 litres per kg of produce
- Annual water savings: approximately 12 million litres vs equivalent soil farm

**Water Recycling:** Nutrient solution is recirculated in a closed-loop NFT system. Evapotranspiration losses are topped up from collected rainwater. Total water recycle rate: 98.2%.

### Energy & Carbon

- **Power source:** 100% renewable — rooftop solar (45%) + purchase agreements for remaining 55% from solar and wind farms in Malaysia
- **Carbon intensity:** 0.042 kg CO₂e per kg produce vs 0.8 kg CO₂e for imported leafy greens (air freight)
- **LED efficiency:** 40% less energy than traditional high-pressure sodium grow lights
- **Carbon offset:** 100% of remaining emissions are offset through verified reforestation projects in Borneo

### Land Use

- Total farm footprint: 500 m² (3-layer vertical)
- Equivalent conventional production would require: ~10,000 m²
- Land use efficiency: **20x improvement**

### Waste Reduction

- **Compostable packaging:** All containers are plant-based bioplastics, compostable within 90 days
- **Crop waste:** <3% of harvest becomes waste — imperfect produce goes to partner food bank
- **Nutrient runoff:** Zero — closed-loop system captures and re-processes all nutrient solution
- **No synthetic pesticides:** IPM (Integrated Pest Management) using beneficial insects (lacewings, predatory mites)

### certifications

- **Singapore Green Building Certification:** GreenMark Gold Plus
- **ISO 14001:** Environmental Management System certified
- **Cold Chain:** SINGAV Cold Chain certification for post-harvest handling
- **Food Safety:** SS 590:2018 HACCP-compliant

### Biodiversity Impact

- Conventional Singapore agriculture has been displaced by urban development
- Vertical farms like Adopt a Kale can produce food without competing for agricultural land
- Growing conditions support beneficial insect populations in urban environment
