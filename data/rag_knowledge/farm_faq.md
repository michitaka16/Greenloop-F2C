# GreenLoop Farm — Frequently Asked Questions

## About Our Farm

**Q: Where is GreenLoop located?**
A: GreenLoop is located 15km from Singapore's CBD in Jurong Innovation District. Our farm spans 500 m² across 3 vertical layers.

**Q: How is GreenLoop different from a conventional farm?**
A: We use hydroponic vertical farming with NFT (Nutrient Film Technique). We grow in stacked layers using 95% less water, zero pesticides, and 20x less land than conventional farming. All produce is harvested and delivered within 24 hours.

**Q: How many crops can you grow?**
A: We currently grow 12 varieties of leafy greens and herbs: spinach, kale, arugula, lettuce, kai lan, basil, cilantro, mint, choy sum, baby bok choy, watercress, and Swiss chard.

## Ordering & Delivery

**Q: What are your minimum order quantities?**
A: Restaurant orders: minimum S$80 per delivery. Retail/personal orders: minimum S$30. We offer free delivery for orders above S$150.

**Q: How quickly can I receive an order?**
A: Same-day delivery for orders placed before 10am. Next-day delivery for orders placed after 10am. We deliver 7 days a week including public holidays.

**Q: What areas do you deliver to?**
A: We deliver throughout mainland Singapore. Jurong, Clementi, Bukit Merah, and CBD areas receive priority morning slots (6am-9am). Remote areas (Lim Chu Kang, Tuas, Sentosa) have afternoon delivery windows.

**Q: How is produce packaged?**
A: All produce comes in compostable bioplastic containers. No plastic wrap. Containers are labeled with harvest date, crop variety, and best-before date. Packaging is 100% compostable within 90 days.

## Product Quality

**Q: How long does hydroponic produce last compared to conventional?**
A: Our produce typically lasts 7-10 days in the refrigerator. This is 30-40% longer than soil-grown equivalents because hydroponic harvesting is gentler and the cold chain is strictly maintained at 4°C.

**Q: Is hydroponic produce as nutritious as soil-grown?**
A: Yes — peer-reviewed studies show hydroponic produce has equivalent or superior nutritional profiles to soil-grown. Our spinach has 30% more iron and 25% more vitamin C than supermarket equivalents based on our internal testing.

**Q: Do you use any pesticides or herbicides?**
A: No pesticides, herbicides, or synthetic chemicals of any kind. We use Integrated Pest Management (IPM) with beneficial insects like predatory mites and lacewings to control any pest populations.

**Q: Are your seeds organic?**
A: Yes, we source non-GMO seeds from certified organic suppliers in the Netherlands and Japan.

## Sustainability

**Q: How much water do you save compared to conventional farming?**
A: Approximately 12 million litres per year compared to equivalent conventional production. That's a 95% reduction.

**Q: Where does your energy come from?**
A: 100% renewable. 45% from our rooftop solar panels, 55% from power purchase agreements with solar and wind farms in Malaysia.

**Q: What happens to produce that doesn't meet your quality standards?**
A: Less than 3% of harvest is rejected. Rejected produce (imperfect appearance, oversize) is donated to our partner food bank, Food Bank Singapore.

## Pricing & Payment

**Q: How is your pricing structured?**
A: We offer volume-based pricing with 3 tiers:
- Standard (1-5 kg/week): S$12-18/kg depending on crop
- Professional (5-20 kg/week): 10% volume discount
- Enterprise (20+ kg/week): custom pricing, dedicated account manager

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Corporate clients: GIRO, bank transfer, PayNow Corporate. Restaurants: we invoice monthly. Retail: credit card, PayNow, GrabPay.

**Q: Do you offer trial/sample orders?**
A: Yes — first-time restaurant clients can order a 2kg mixed sample box for S$35 (regularly S$50). Retail customers can try our "Fresh Start Box" (500g mixed greens) for S$15.
